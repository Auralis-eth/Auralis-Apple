**The checklist, the per-area read ledger, and the cumulative findings register.**

The register is **cumulative and never deleted** — when a finding is fixed, its Status becomes
`Resolved (Round N)` and the original finding text stays intact. The **Review Ledger**'s notes column
is where durable carry-forward invariants live, and is the highest-value part of this audit for a
fresh reader: it records what must *not* be "fixed".
